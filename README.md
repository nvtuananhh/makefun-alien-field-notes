# Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review

One line: a review of planet Earth left by an alien tourist.

> The locals pay $40 an hour to be shouted at while running in place, then $30 to sit in hot water. Four stars; the sunsets are free.

They pay to be shouted at while running nowhere, then pay to sit in hot water — and the best thing on the planet costs nothing.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` when run.

Gate: one line, 1–200 UTF-8 characters, verified over three runs with identical output.
